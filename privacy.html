Privacy policy

Your rental records

RC Roomkeep is provided by Rizk Corsight, LLC. Property names, room labels, dates, notes and original photographs are stored in the app’s local storage. No Roomkeep account is required. We do not operate a server that receives your rental records or sell your records for advertising.

Photos, camera and reminders

You choose the photos and files to import and when to use the camera. Original photographs can include location and other embedded metadata. Record dates come from you or your device and are not independently verified. Optional reminders use your device’s notification services.

Exports and backups

When you export or share, the destination you choose receives the selected report, photograph or backup. That destination may be a cloud provider and has its own privacy practices. Portable ZIP backups are not encrypted by RC Roomkeep and may include original photo metadata. Review the contents and destination before sharing. Android automatic app backup is disabled. Apple device backups may include app data according to your device settings.

Purchases and platform services

Apple StoreKit or Google Play Billing provides product information, purchase processing and restoration. These providers process purchase, device and service information under their own terms and privacy policies. RC Roomkeep stores local trial and access status and does not receive your payment-card details. On Android, Google Play Billing transmits diagnostic events such as billing API successes, failures and connection information, with a billing-client session identifier, app and SDK versions, and device characteristics such as model, manufacturer, memory and operating-system build. Google uses this information to operate, support and improve billing services. This is separate from your rental records. If you choose to leave a store review, the store processes and may publish your rating and review under its own terms. Starting the local three-day trial does not create a subscription or authorize a purchase.

Demo workspace

The public demo uses a separate local workspace with fictional sample records. It does not unlock or change your personal purchase or trial status. You can reset the demo separately from your personal records. Only import information into the demo that you are comfortable using there.

Keeping and deleting data

You can delete records and photos within the app. Uninstalling or clearing app data can remove local records; export a backup first. Files you exported elsewhere remain at their destinations until you delete them there. We cannot restore records that were stored only on your device.

Support and contact

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you send so we can respond. Do not send private property records or original photographs unless necessary for your request. Contact rizkcorsight@rizkcorsight.com for support or privacy questions.
