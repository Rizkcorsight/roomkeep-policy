RC Roomkeep support

Keep rental condition photos and notes organized from move-in to move-out.

Start a record

Choose to start the three-day trial, create a dated rental record, then add rooms, photos and notes. Use separate records for move-in, routine checks and move-out. Compare selected photos from different records and export a PDF or text report.

Keep a portable backup

Export a ZIP backup before changing devices, uninstalling or clearing app data. Restore that file on iOS or Android. A report is a summary; use a complete backup to preserve your original photos and records.

One-time full access

The three-day trial begins only when you choose. Afterward an optional one-time purchase unlocks full access. There is no subscription or automatic renewal. Existing records, original photos and backup exports remain accessible after the trial. Use Restore Purchases with the same store account to restore an eligible purchase. Store purchases are platform-specific.

If something goes wrong

Do not uninstall as a first troubleshooting step. If the app reports a recovery problem, preserve existing files and contact support before making changes. Include your device model, OS version, app version and a description of the issue. Avoid attaching private photos or complete backups unless needed.
